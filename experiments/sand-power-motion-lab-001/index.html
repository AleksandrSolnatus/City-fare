ACTIVE EXPERIMENT

Equations and limits

Fixed timestep Δt = 1/60 s. θ ← θ + ωΔt; head ← head + v(cos θ, sin θ)Δt. Each following segment relaxes toward a fixed-spacing target behind its predecessor, with response 1 − exp(−kΔt). Camera follows the head with response 1 − exp(−cΔt).

Coverage is a visual mask from chosen depth plus a fixed sinusoidal dune field. Tracks are temporary screen marks, not persistent terrain deformation. Rider is a scale dot. No physical sand, collision, momentum or riding animation is claimed.

Independent experiment

FIRST PASS

Let the head lead.

Hold speed fixed. Compare turn rates 0.35 and 0.9 rad/s, then vary body response alone. Watch whether the tail follows naturally or cuts across the turn.

Success: a full turn, coherent body curve, rider still readable, and a camera that preserves speed cues.

Failure reveals: a steering limit, follower lag, spacing collapse, or camera motion hiding speed. This toy can suggest a control change; test it in the real build.
